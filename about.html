Our Story

George College of Management and Science, a part of the George Group of Colleges, George College of Management and science,established in 2003, offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs, with a focus on practical learning and career preparation through placements and internships.

Affiliation:

George College of Management and Science is affiliated with the Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT).

Location:

The college has campuses near Taratala, Maheshtala, and Budge Budge Trunk Road in South West Kolkata.

Programs:

They offer full-time Bachelor Degree Courses in various professional fields, including BBA, BCA, BHM, B.OPTOM. and B.Sc. in Medical Lab Technology.

History:

The George Telegraph Training Institute, which later became George College, was established in 1920 to provide job-oriented training.

Infrastructure:

The college has a main campus with classrooms, tutorial rooms, seminar halls, and a multipurpose hall, as well as an annex building with administrative offices and a student's common room.

Our Focus

The college emphasizes practical learning through industry visits, workshops, and seminars. They offer both on-campus and off-campus recruitment opportunities for students. The training and placement cell provides internship and employment opportunities. Students are prepared for placements through practice sessions, group discussions, mock interviews, and soft skills grooming sessions. Placements:

Our Placement

Placement statistics vary, with some sources indicating a placement rate of above around 70%. The highest package offered is 15 LPA, the lowest is 5 LPA, and the average is 3 LPA. Some students have been placed at companies like Royal Enfield and Make MyTrip.
